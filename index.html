Job Route

Distance, courses and time by water — follows channels and goes around headlands and rocks. Figure Eight Pools to Manly, the harbour's eastern bays, Botany Bay to Tom Uglys Bridge and Port Hacking.

      Fuel & Range

      For long jobs: an extended search, a tow to Botany Bay, or a job outside the usual patrol area. It tells you how long you can stay out and how far you can go before you must turn back, keeping a third in reserve (rule of thirds: ⅓ out, ⅓ back, ⅓ reserve). Towing and heavy seas burn more — use a higher burn rate. Tank capacity 450 L.

      2 NM Boundary

      Alarm off.

      Measured from the nearest land, island, rock platform or reef (OpenStreetMap). Warns 500 m before the limit and again beyond it. Works while the app is open on screen. An aid only — the plotter is the authority.

      Phone Setup Check

      The app needs the microphone for voice entries, location for positions and the 2 NM alarm, and notifications for boundary alerts.

        If something says “Blocked”

        iPhone / iPad (Safari): Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Safari Websites → While Using. Then in Safari, tap aA in the address bar → Website Settings → Microphone and Location → Allow. For notifications, add the app to the Home Screen first (Share → Add to Home Screen), open it from the icon, then tap Allow access.

        Android (Chrome): tap the icon left of the address → Permissions → turn on Microphone, Location and Notifications. Also check Android Settings → Location is on and Chrome is allowed to use it.

        After changing a setting, close and reopen the app.

        Distress, urgency & safety calls

        MAYDAY — grave and imminent danger

        1. MAYDAY, MAYDAY, MAYDAY
        2. This is [vessel name], [vessel name], [vessel name]
        3. MAYDAY [vessel name]
        4. My position is [lat/long, or range & bearing from a landmark]
        5. Nature of distress [sinking, fire, person overboard…]
        6. Assistance required
        7. Number of persons on board
        8. Any other information [vessel description, intentions]
        9. OVER

        PAN-PAN — urgent, not life-threatening

        PAN-PAN ×3, ALL STATIONS ×3, this is [vessel] ×3, position, nature of urgency, assistance required, persons on board, OVER.

        SÉCURITÉ — navigation or weather safety

        SÉCURITÉ ×3, ALL STATIONS ×3, this is [vessel] ×3, safety message, OUT.

        Channels

        VHF Ch 16 — distress, urgency, safety & calling. Confirm local working channels with the skipper.
